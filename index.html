Engine physics simulation

This mode runs Ange Yaghi's engine-sim: real pistons, valves, gas flow and exhaust pulses, simulated thousands of times a second on your phone. It keeps one CPU core busy the whole time, so expect the phone to get warm and the battery to drain faster. Older or budget phones may not keep up, and you'll hear gaps. Big engines (10+ cylinders) are the heaviest.

Standard mode is our tuned synth: light, smooth, and it runs on anything.

Physics mode: heavy on CPU and battery. Watch the CPU % in the corner; step down quality in Build → Advanced if the sound gaps.
